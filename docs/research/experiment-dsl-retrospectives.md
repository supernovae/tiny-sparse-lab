# Experiment plan retrospectives

Retrospective views are read-only interpretations of a resolved, identity-bound plan and, when present, its separately verified evidence index. They do not reconstruct an old campaign by pretending its records were authored under `plan_version: 1`. The five archived examples below are **historical records**, not executable new locks; original identity conventions and preregistration timing remain independent of present-day schema compatibility. A checksummed record can establish a bounded historical fact even when the underlying ignored checkpoint files are not locally available. Availability is not historical certainty. No view automatically promotes a checkpoint, baseline, or research claim; promotion requires the separately reviewed lifecycle declaration described in [evidence and reporting](evidence-and-reporting.md).

The machine view API is `sparselab.experiments.retrospective.retrospective_views(lock, *, workspace=None, index_path=None) -> dict`. Pass a `ResolvedExperimentPlan` from `resolve_plan` for planned-only output, or a published lock reopened with `open_lock` and both its experiment workspace and an explicit content-addressed evidence index path to consume verified collected evidence. Every view carries facts and separately labelled inferences; `pending`, `failed`, `ingestion_error`, `invalid` and `unavailable` are not successful evidence. The JSON `format` is `sparselab-experiment-retrospective-v1`. The five stable view names are `identity_provenance`, `contrast_interventions`, `phased_lineage`, `evidence_coverage`, and `readiness_decision`. `collect` must verify worker/spec/plan identities before a completed cell counts; a plan alone never establishes effectiveness. Absence of a collected index is a planned-only state, not a negative scientific outcome.

## 1. Identity and provenance — dense-lm-v1

**Archival facts:** [`artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_v1.json`](../../artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_v1.json) identifies three seeds (17, 42, 73), checkpoint chains and a 4,096-update endpoint. [`configs/dense_lm_v1_lifecycle.json`](../../configs/dense_lm_v1_lifecycle.json) and the [lifecycle record](dense-lm-v1.md) establish the bounded reference's original protocol and reviewed status. The lifecycle documentation states that exact pre-run raw hashes of that lifecycle config and research document were not retained in the immutable stage bundle. **Inference/limit:** do not invent a git commit or an `experiments/research/dense-lm-v1/` directory; this original campaign is not an exact modern `ResolvedExperimentPlan` merely because its report is intact. A historically identified checkpoint need not be currently runnable.

## 2. Contrast and interventions — dense-lm-scale-v1 and data-rich

**Archival facts:** [`dense-lm-scale-v1/evidence.json`](../../experiments/research/dense-lm-scale-v1/evidence.json) binds its three per-seed 30M-versus-50M references and 50M run manifests; its recorded dimensions are 29,893,120 versus 50,274,752 parameters, 16,777,216 supervised target exposures, and ROCm. Its [protocol](../../experiments/research/dense-lm-scale-v1/protocol.md) defines the matched architecture contrast. A separately recorded [data-rich run](../../experiments/research/tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1/evidence.json) changed source/data, tokenizer, context, schedule and precision together; its common-text bits/UTF-8-byte panel is not its native-tokenizer held-out target loss. **Inference/limit:** the data-rich comparison is multi-factor, not a single-factor data ablation. Lower loss alone cannot establish model quality or causal superiority; the scale study reports mixed generation behavior and higher time/storage costs. Do not attribute any data-rich change to one factor.

## 3. Phased lineage — dense-lm-token-budget-v1

**Archival facts:** [`artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_token_budget_v1.json`](../../artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_token_budget_v1.json) records original 4,096-update full-state parents, continuations to 8,192 and 16,384 updates, and original decay-horizon control. Its `preserved_interrupted_attempt` records the rejected seed-42 partial 8,192 child separately from the corrected accepted child. The original parent was not replaced by the later milestone. **Inference/limit:** a milestone claim requires its selected immutable checkpoint and bound ancestry; a mutable `latest` alias or an interrupted attempt is not the accepted child. No extended checkpoint was automatically promoted. See the [plain-language review](dense-lm-token-budget-review.md).

## 4. Evidence coverage — dense-lm-decoding-v1

**Archival facts:** [`dense-lm-decoding-v1/evidence/summary.json`](../../experiments/research/dense-lm-decoding-v1/evidence/summary.json) records six source checkpoint arms, a development selection kept apart from independent test prompts, and a prepared 198-pair blinded review with `votes: 0` and `status: UNAVAILABLE`. The [results](../../experiments/research/dense-lm-decoding-v1/results.md) describe the 990 test cells. **Inference/limit:** prepared blinded pairs do not imply completed human review or preference; zero votes mean subjective quality is unavailable, not negative. No new training occurred in this decoding experiment.

## 5. Readiness and decision — tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1

**Archival facts:** [`tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1/evidence.json`](../../experiments/research/tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1/evidence.json) identifies the seed-42 terminal generation at update 24,576 and 100,663,296 target exposures. Its same-raw-text panel records 0.668729/0.975226/0.955759 bits per UTF-8 byte for new/old-30M/old-50M respectively. The [research roadmap](roadmap.md) retains this candidate unpromoted, notes contradictions in prompts, and separates independent prose preference as an open evidence question. **Inference/limit:** even an intact endpoint and a favorable common-text compression measure are insufficient to infer human preference, independent replication, or promotion; its ignored run/checkpoint availability must be checked separately from the checked-in receipt.

For active locks, `identity_provenance` exposes locked hashes/config/source/input identities, `contrast_interventions` exposes every declared diff and control mode, `phased_lineage` distinguishes locked selectors from observed bound generations, `evidence_coverage` separates actual verified outcomes by cell/evaluation role, and `readiness_decision` states missing gates and the boundary on any decision. These views cannot substitute post-hoc triage for preregistered evaluation or silently convert descriptive smoke runs into effectiveness claims.
