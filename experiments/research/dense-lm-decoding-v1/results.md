The 660 **development** cells ran greedy (`temperature=0`, `top_k=0`) once and both supported non-greedy alternatives (`temperature=0.8`, `top_k=0` versus `temperature=0.8`, `top_k=40`) at RNG seeds 11 and 29 for every prompt/checkpoint. Nucleus/top-p/min-p are not supported by the existing generator and were not added. The preregistered mechanical lexicographic rule first compared empty/pathological output counts, then narrow explicit-anchor contradictions, then repeated-trigram and repeated-bigram rates per emitted token. Both candidate policies had 0 empty/pathological cells and **1** flagged explicit contradiction in development. Untruncated temperature sampling had **135 / 11,444** repeated trigrams (1.180 per 100 tokens) and **470 / 11,444** repeated bigrams (4.107 per 100), versus top-k 40's **161 / 11,355** (1.418) and **555 / 11,355** (4.888). Thus the frozen rule selected **`temperature_0_8`** before any test generation. The [selection receipt](evidence/selection.json) SHA-256 is `141a7efb4f7f022b4bad7d95895bb05fc7e968468d7eda7060095d3fadc9b1f6`. This was a repetition-oriented screen, **not** human assessment that the chosen decoder writes better stories.

## Regression view: keep the old extraction policy

`regression_decoder` remains the original temperature-zero/top-k-zero, **32-token** six-prompt panel from the earlier [scale results](../dense-lm-scale-v1/results.md). Its raw three-seed matched checkpoint observations are referenced by SHA-256 `2533446ea7b6cd13524853a703493cb78ebac9b9b287e948f92ae096c1809db9`; neither it nor its decoder was rerun with modified settings or used to select this study's policy. Under that fixed extraction policy, the prior study found a lower held-out LM loss for 50M at each seed but **mixed** six-case stories: green-meadow contradictions disappear in two seeds while boat, wet-path, and bread continuity are inconsistent elsewhere. That prior Finding stands unchanged. The deterministic view answers *what changed with the weights under fixed extraction*, not how pleasant an alternative sample can be.

Greedy was also included on the **new 55 prompts** as a distinct matched control with a 48-token horizon. It is *not* the original regression panel. The comparison below is therefore within the new test population, not a redefined regression gate.

## Independent quality-view evidence: selected sampling versus greedy

After the selection receipt was sealed, all six test checkpoints ran once on the 55 previously ungenerated prompts: one greedy output plus two prespecified samples (RNG 11 and 29) of the selected policy per prompt. The **990 complete cells** record exact prompt, full output and completion, decoder settings, sampled seed or `null` for greedy, checkpoint/run identity, exact emitted token IDs and count, diagnostics and elapsed time in the six [test JSONL files](evidence/test/). No favorable completion was picked; the 660 development cells and all competing top-k outputs are separately archived in [development JSONL](evidence/development/). Failed/empty rows would have remained visible; every planned cell was present and all 990 succeeded mechanically.

Counts and denominators are *emitted decoder token IDs*, not a re-encoding of displayed text. Early EOS includes natural endings and premature termination and is **not** a defect count. All arms had zero empty and zero emitted special-token cases; the narrow explicit checker spotted one actual error: on `test-color-attribute-continuity-05`, 50M/seed17/sample11 turned the explicitly *red bucket* into a “**blue bucket**.” A checker that only matches adjacent color-and-object words misses many subtle contradictions, omitted entities, misplaced objects and causal failures. Its zero is not proof of continuity.

On matched prompt/checkpoint pairs, sampling lowered repeated bigram excess for **256/330** 30M comparisons and **230/330** 50M comparisons (raised it in 47 and 59; the rest tied). Repeated trigram excess fell in **210/330** and **192/330** respectively (rose in 31 and 46). The five categories `named_character_continuity`, `color_attribute_continuity`, `location_permanence`, `ordinary_continuation` and `unusual_grammatical` each show lower pooled trigram rates under sampling for both widths; all eleven category-level counts are in [summary.json](evidence/summary.json). These are repeated observations over only three *trained* seeds, not 330 independent model replications.

Less repetition **did not establish better stories**. A predetermined, first-per-category test example at seed 42: the prompt says “A green ribbon was tied around the box. The ribbon was green as the child”; 30M greedy continues with a repetitive **box** story, while the sampled continuation abruptly introduces a **mouse** and “B miser washing.” For “The baker stored the flour in the pantry. At dawn, she went to the pantry to fetch the”, 30M greedy starts “soup” and loops on cake, whereas sampled text starts “ie's Johnny” and drifts through a milk/pipe scene. On the 50M version of the first prompt, greedy switches to a box-search dialogue and the sample again introduces a mouse. These examples were chosen by prompt ID **before** inspecting outcomes; full completions, including 29-seed alternatives and less dramatic cases, remain in the evidence. Fewer repeated n-grams may simply exchange looping for incoherent/entity-drifting text. There was no post-test move to top-k or a revised temperature.

For **50M versus 30M under the selected decoder**, 330 matched prompt/training-seed/sampling-seed pairs had lower bigram excess in the 50M arm **117** times, higher **134**, and tied **79**; lower trigram excess **71**, higher **82**, tied **177**. Token repetition and length also have mixed signs ([matched-pair counts](evidence/summary.json)). One mechanically explicit color contradiction appears only in the 50M sampled arm. This independent prompt set does **not** demonstrate that the larger model provides more coherent sampled prose; human subjective model-quality difference remains **UNAVAILABLE**. It also cannot prove equal quality or generalize beyond these prompts, sampling seeds, checkpoints and 48-token horizon.

## Blinded review and cost

The deterministic [blind bundle](evidence/review-blind.json) has **198** matched pairs (22 preselected prompts × three training seeds × model comparison and two within-model decoder comparisons), with A/B order mixed and model width, seed and policy absent from reviewer rows. It is bound by SHA-256 `ced34f6f4e163f4c61691d8d5ccde0f0b235a5b9350781e7782256f98126f8e8`. The separate key remains in ignored task-owned storage; [review.md](review.md) gives six independent pairwise dimensions, tie/uncertain options and an obligation to retain disagreements. **No human reviewed any pair in this run** (0 votes). Blinding materials are prepared, not evidence of quality judgments. Subjective quality, entity/causal correctness beyond limited obvious checks and human preference rates are explicitly **UNAVAILABLE**; held-out LM loss must not substitute for them.

The task workspace `sparselab-work/experiments/dense-lm-decoding-v1/` retains original exclusive-write development/test observations, frozen selection and private review key. Archived JSONL is identical byte-for-byte to that workspace ([hash ledger](evidence/summary.json)); prior training runs/checkpoints were not written. On the same GPU the new test generations spent **485.34 s** in synchronized generation calls (42,691 emitted tokens across both widths/policies) plus loading, hash checking and process/report overhead; the six-command test-and-bundle process wall was **524.58 s**. Development calls took **315.45 s** (28,281 tokens), versus **51.67 + 300.29 s** command wall across the first and remaining commands. These are inference timing only, not training update times or a causal decoder speed benchmark. Approximate per-test arm generation throughput ranged 78–110 emitted tokens/s across the particular model/decoder/host measurements; peak measured inference allocated/reserved VRAM after each checkpoint load was about **206/220 MB (30M)** and **287/321 MB (50M)**, not the peak training allocator. These observations cannot be extrapolated to different concurrency/hardware.

The separate [runtime receipt](evidence/runtime.json) records all six measured inference allocator peaks and command-wall boundaries; [summary.json](evidence/summary.json) binds synchronized per-cell generation durations to raw outputs.

## Bounded disposition

**Decoding artifact:** substantial n-gram and sentence-loop differences under unchanged weights establish that some visible repetition is decoder-sensitive. **Model/data limitation remains plausible:** severe prompt/entity drift and ungainly fragments persist or appear under sampling, but a mechanical repetition improvement does not partition their causes cleanly. **Capacity:** the prior lower 50M held-out loss survives as a separate learning result; the independent sample gives mixed mechanical differences and no demonstrated subjective advantage for either width. **Sampling tradeoff:** lower repetition can coincide with explicit color contradiction and incoherence. No universally best decoder, no revised regression decoder, no new baseline promotion and no claim that 50M is a better independent storyteller. A future blinded human review of the sealed bundle and, if warranted, a newly preregistered decoder-selection study—not reuse of this opened test set for tuning—would be needed to answer the subjective-quality question.
