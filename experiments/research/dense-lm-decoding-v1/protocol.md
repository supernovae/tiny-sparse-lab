# dense-lm-decoding-v1: frozen inference-only comparison

## Question and boundaries

Which visible continuation failures change when the *same immutable learned weights* are decoded differently, and does the mature 50.27M fully dense decoder differ from the mature 29.89M fully dense decoder on independent prompts? No new training, optimizer state update, weight promotion, dataset preparation, altered checkpoint, or change to either predecessor study. The three like-seeded 16,384-step / 16,777,216-target terminal pairs (42, 17, 73) from [token-budget evidence](../../../artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_token_budget_v1.json) and [scale evidence](../dense-lm-scale-v1/evidence.json) are the only models. This is a comparison of matched *seed labels* and budgets, not shared initial weights across widths. Both use the verified TinyStories tokenizer and data provenance, PyTorch ROCm FP32 on the available device. The 30M extension has an earlier original-source revision; report this limitation, not bitwise source equivalence.

## Two surfaces that cannot be merged

**Regression view:** `regression_decoder` is the existing 32-token, temperature-zero, top-k-zero greedy decoder on [the six immutable regression prompts](../../../data/dense_lm_v1_prompts.json). Existing [scale-study observations](../dense-lm-scale-v1/evidence.json) for all three matched mature endpoint pairs are the read-only reference. Do not reselect prompts, alter generation code semantics, tune on this panel, or replace the decoder because another produces nicer text. It asks whether the *weights* changed under fixed extraction; it does not rank available prose quality.

**Quality view:** separately authored [development](development.json) (22 prompts, two per category) and untouched [test](test.json) (55 prompts, five per category) cover entity/named-character/object/color continuity; temporal order, cause/effect, object permanence/location; ordinary continuation, dialogue, natural ending, and unusual-but-grammatical stories. Mix ordinary and stress cases. Both are authored in this campaign, not taken from the six prompts; they are not proved absent from TinyStories training. Record their raw file hashes before generation. Do not generate *any* test completion until the non-greedy decoder is chosen and the choice recorded. The only variation between matched inference cells is decoder policy and model checkpoint, with two prespecified sampling RNG seeds. The output horizon is 48 new tokens, no stop sequences, strict 256-token context, cache-enabled, identical tokenizer and exact prompt bytes. Early EOS is retained, never rerun for a preferred ending.

## Supported policies and preregistered selection

The existing PyTorch decoder supports greedy `temperature=0, top_k=0` (seed irrelevant), unconstrained temperature sampling `temperature=0.8, top_k=0`, and moderate top-k sampling `temperature=0.8, top_k=40`. No nucleus/top-p or min-p is currently implemented: adding a new strategy is not necessary to isolate the proposed decoder question and would change the inference implementation separately. `generate_with_token_ids` only exposes the existing decoder's exact emitted IDs without changing its `generate` output. Freeze source digest and smoke-test greedy equivalence before collecting evidence. For development, run greedy once and **both** sampled policies at generation seeds 11 and 29, for all 22 prompts and all six retained checkpoints (660 planned cells). For test, run greedy once and only the already-selected non-greedy policy at generation seeds 11 and 29, for all 55 prompts and six checkpoints (990 planned cells). Do not select a generation seed, prompt, model, or other parameter based on pleasant output; report failed/missing cells explicitly.

Choose exactly one non-greedy policy using *only* development mechanical observations pooled across the **six checkpoints and two generation seeds**. Minimize the lexicographic tuple `(empty-or-pathological-output count, explicit anchor-contradiction count, repeated-trigram excess / emitted tokens, repeated-bigram excess / emitted tokens)`; a zero-token output contributes to the first count and zero to repetition denominators. Ties choose `top_k_40` before `temperature_0_8` as the more bounded distribution. The selected policy is a mechanically screened **quality-decoder candidate**, not evidence of subjective prose superiority; report the tuple components and cases per checkpoint/category too, not just a pooled number. Never use the test set, fixed regression panel or a favorable sampled story for selection. Preserve the selection receipt and hash it before accessing test output. No retroactive policy changes.

## Evidence, diagnostics and review

Every observed cell records: full prompt, category and explicit anchors, run/checkpoint digest and step, policy name/parameters, RNG seed (`null` for greedy), full text and completion, exact emitted token IDs/count (EOS excluded), elapsed generation seconds and device identity. Retain failures and attempted cells, do not silently replace them. Count token/bigram/trigram excess from **emitted IDs**, duplicate complete sentences, premature empty output, special tokens and generated length separately. Limited explicit checks may flag contradictory color-object pairs and overt renaming or relabeling where an anchor is mechanically specified in a prompt. Unrecognized wording, a missing mention, and an introduced new character do not prove a contradiction. These rules have false negatives and cannot certify causal consistency, readability or story quality. Report all 11 categories and matched seed/model/policy differences rather than declaring an aggregate quality winner.

Prepare a blinded pairwise review bundle from a prespecified deterministic 22-prompt subset of *test* (two prompt IDs per category by sorted ID) after all outputs are sealed: matched 30M versus 50M under the selected decoder and matched greedy versus selected decoder per model. Hide model width, training seed, decoder identity and generation seed; randomize A/B by digest of pair identifier, preserve an external key and all text. Separate votes for prompt adherence, entities, stated attributes, temporal/causal coherence, repetition and local readability; allow tie/uncertain and reviewer disagreement, never force consensus. No participant is currently committed; if nobody reviews, record subjective quality and human text-quality difference as **UNAVAILABLE**, not as LM-loss or a mechanical proxy. A reviewer must not see the key before judgments. Keep original test outputs and the key under task-owned `sparselab-work/experiments/dense-lm-decoding-v1/`; retain a small review protocol and blinded bundle/evidence references in this study.

## Interpretation and cost

Present the fixed regression view independently from the frozen selected-decoder test view. Compare within-checkpoint decoder deltas before cross-model comparisons; inspect prompt-specific repetition/drift, per-category cases, seed and sampling variability and disagreement. A lower repetition count can coincide with worse continuity. If both decoders fail, model/data/training limitations remain plausible but not proven; if 50M has fewer mechanical defects on untouched prompts, this is bounded evidence, **not** independent subjective text-quality superiority absent review. No significance or broad generalization claim from 55 handcrafted prompts and three trained seeds. Record runtime/peak device memory and failures/unavailable values separately from model quality. Freeze this document, both prompt files, runner and checkpoint/provenance identities in `preregistration.json` before any development generation; write observed results in `results.md` and durable per-cell evidence outside frozen inputs.
