# DevMind v2 web and academic source map

**Protocol identity:** `source-map-web-academic-v1`, researched 2026-09-29. **Status:** candidate research only. No dataset was downloaded, sampled into Corpus Forge, tokenized, counted as eligible, or approved for redistribution. Repository API metadata, publisher-stated token totals and publisher-listed compressed-file sizes below are **upstream observations**, not local measurements. Budgets, expected token yield and split assignments are **proposals**, not acquisition receipts. A public source with known provenance and no explicit incompatible training prohibition may be considered for *private training* after screening; permission to redistribute raw text or publish weights is a separate determination. Retain exact artifact identity, notices, attribution and denial reasons for every admitted record. No human per-file legal attestation is assumed.

## Immediate source decision and bounded first pass

1. **Primary scale candidate:** [FineWeb-Edu](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu/raw/87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9/README.md), HF revision `87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9`, preferably one [named `sample/10BT/000_00000.parquet` shard (2,152,819,114 compressed bytes)](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu/tree/87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9/sample/10BT). The publisher calls its *whole sample* ~10B GPT-2 tokens, not any one shard. One shard is an economical **prospective** 250M+ source-token candidate, not proof: actual retained, cross-source-deduplicated tokens must be measured with the subsequently frozen DevMind tokenizer. A sample shard mixes Common Crawl snapshots and cannot itself implement disjoint crawl-family validation/test; prefer separately pinned `data/CC-MAIN-YYYY-NN/*.parquet` partitions when freezing independent families. The sample is nested inside the 100BT/350BT samples: **do not sum or simultaneously train on these as independent data**.
2. **Independent subject supplements, not a requirement to obtain the scale:** [English Wikibooks `20260901` articles multistream](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwikibooks/20260901/) (197.7 MB compressed plus 1.5 MB index); selected [Wikipedia `20260901` split multistream files](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwiki/20260901/) (~286–600 MB each, not the 25.0 GB combined dump); and selected NIST first-party technical series. Count only content genuinely useful to the developer/systems scope; broad encyclopedic content cannot be silently relabeled technical. Retain publisher-approved credit and page/revision/source IDs. Wikipedia/wikibooks copyleft and third-party exceptions warrant separate raw-publication decisions.
3. **Alternative crawl pool, not additive by default:** [DCLM Baseline Parquet](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mlfoundations/dclm-baseline-1.0-parquet/blob/817d6752765f6a41261085171dd546b104f60626/README.md) one ~144 MB shard, plus other shards **only if** complementary domain coverage survives URL/content dedup with FineWeb-Edu. Its own card calls the collection a **research-only baseline** and not production-ready; defer deployment-aimed reuse until that scope is resolved. [OpenWebMath](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-web-math/open-web-math/blob/fde8ef8de2300f5e778f56261843dab89f230815/README.md) selected mathematical Parquet shards may fill math gaps but count overlaps with both crawls once. A roughly 2–5 GB initial compressed download is a *budget suggestion* for a FineWeb shard plus selective independent sources, **not** a measured corpus size or guaranteed token yield; stop/add reviewed shards based on actual filtered unique-token count. No multi-TB default snapshot.

**Scale arithmetic, not a token measurement:** the pinned [`sample/10BT` listing](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu/tree/87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9/sample/10BT) contains 14 shards (13 around 2.15 GB, one ~0.54 GB). Dividing the publisher's ~10B GPT-2 token *sample-wide* label by 14 gives a **rough ~714M GPT-2 tokens per shard on average**. Distribution between shards, filtration, canonical-source precedence, rights exclusions and selected-tokenizer segmentation are unknown: even the proposed 250M+ *retained* distinct-source-token target is a gate to measure, not a claim. A 2.15 GB shard plus 197.7 MB Wikibooks and two ~300–600 MB Wikipedia split files remains far below a multi-TB download.

The existing [v1 source survey](../devmind-pretrain-v1/source-survey.md) records 23,155,632 selected bytes in v0 and unresolved per-file rights/duplicate questions; none of the candidate web sources below retrospectively licenses that release. A corpus adapter that blindly downloads all allowed HF shards is unsuitable for the proposed **single-shard** bounds: supply a pinned exact-file allowlist and verify the stored file checksum/size before declaring acquisition. Wikimedia XML/bzip2 and peS2o gzip/zstd are not Parquet; require a separately reviewed bounded normalizer/acquisition path rather than mislabeling their formats.

- **Dolma:** [Dolma dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/dolma/blob/f606534e4f82123943f89813eb4f159129a88d60/README.md) separates web, scholarly, encyclopedia, books, code and social sources; explicitly requires respecting original-source agreements despite its dataset-level ODC-By. Borrow its explicit source-mixture disclosure and independent per-origin screening.

## Family and contamination protocol before any ingestion

1. **Identity hierarchy and precedence:** normalize and retain canonical URL (strip fragments/tracking, preserve significant query and publication date), source record ID, crawl dump/WARC origin, page/paper/RFC ID and revision, publisher and content hash. A pinned **direct authoritative** Wikimedia page/first-party NIST document/individually rights-reviewed paper takes precedence over any crawler copy; retain crawl provenance as rejected duplicate evidence. Same URL across FineWeb/DCLM/OpenWebMath and repeated crawl dates counts **once** for distinct-content supply unless substantially revised; exact normalized-content SHA followed by near-duplicate shingle/MinHash detection for mirrored passages/quotations/templated pages. Same Semantic Scholar/arXiv/DOI record or wiki page across releases is one family. Never dedup away stronger direct-source rights evidence in favor of a crawler's dataset-wide license.
2. **Train/validation/test are source families, not row-random shard partitions:** reserve whole Common Crawl `dump` IDs *and* URL hosts/site families (assign overlapping hosts only one split) before selection; the FineWeb 10BT sample must expose `dump`/`url` and cannot guarantee partition completeness. Assign whole Wikibooks book roots and Wikipedia category/parent-topic/page-ID groups disjointly; reserve whole NIST report series/DOIs, peS2o paper IDs/venues and publication cohorts, OpenWebMath domains, and RFC series/numbered standards for withheld evaluation. Example proposal: 2023-50 and 2024-10 crawl train, 2024-18 validation, 2024-26 sealed test **only if all named dumps exist at the pin and pass rights/quantity checks**; book/report/paper families partitioned separately. Never use upstream `train` labels as DevMind test-isolation proof; keep test entirely out of tokenizer selection.
3. **Evaluation contamination:** freeze prompt-set parent documents and public benchmark/title/solution fingerprints before any fit. Remove from training any URL, canonical article, scientific-paper ID, NIST section, duplicated paragraph, paraphrased source passage or quoted answer intersecting held-out cases; audit cross-split near-duplicates including cross-origin copies. Crawl mirrors of Kubernetes/CPython/Go and wiki/scientific articles are not new independent evidence. Report false positives and surviving near-duplicate examples rather than claim universal semantic dedup. Sealed developer/system cards require independent world/template families even if web corpus is huge.
4. **Rights gates and measurement:** archive publisher source URLs, terms revision/access date, repo SHA/dump job+file checksum, per-record no-ML/terms exclusions, source-family membership, attribution, redistribution class (`raw_redistributable` only after terms/embedded-media check; otherwise `metadata_reconstruction_only`) and separate weight-publication assessment. Record compressed bytes actually fetched, extracted/eligible bytes, documents by source/family, origin/shape/verification, and tokenizer-selected **distinct eligible train tokens** only after acquisition and tokenization. All estimated yields here remain unverified; no token target is satisfied by source-label totals or reusing another dataset's published counts.
