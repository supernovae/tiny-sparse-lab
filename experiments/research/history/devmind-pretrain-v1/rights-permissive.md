## Exclusions and release gates

- **Known licensed exceptions ≠ unknown rights.** CPython O'Malley, Purcell, MagicStack and Taneli Hukkinen notices have specific stated rights/attribution to review and carry if included. OTel first-party Go examples `service/service.go`, `config/confighttp/client.go`, `receiver/otlpreceiver/config.go`, `exporter/debugexporter/exporter.go` each start `Copyright The OpenTelemetry Authors` and `SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0`; this **sample** is not an all-file ownership audit. Its `examples/*.yaml`, docs and schemas do not show that header in their observed openings. GPL/copyleft candidates elsewhere in the v1 survey (Git/Linux) are **licensed with obligations**, not automatically illegal or unknown; they are outside this permissive-source path proposal and need their separate rights decision. Unknown upstream snippets, linked text, vendored dependencies, testimonials/attributions and fixture provenance remain **unknown pending review**, not silently labeled first-party.
- Explicitly reject unscreened `vendor/**`, `third_party/**`, `**/testdata/**` except reviewed, named text fixtures, generated schemas/bundles/tables unless separately justified, binaries, images/figures, certificates, keys, crash dumps, personal data and externally quoted examples. The rejected path can be legitimately licensed yet excluded for provenance, duplication, privacy or quality. Do not infer blanket OTel clearance from Apache root, Go from BSD root, docs from CC BY root or Python from PSF root.
- Preserve URI/full revision/file path, origin author and every applicable file-level copyright, notice and license in a per-file rights ledger; record extraction changes (especially CC BY attribution/change notice and Apache changed-file notices). **Training eligibility, permission to redistribute source text, and implications for model weights are separate decisions**; no automatic model-weight licensing conclusion follows from a repository license or this inspection. Obtain project-specific legal/policy review for intended training, source export and weight publication; do not call unknown third-party material `redistributable` to pass a gate.
- Nine-kind coverage is still blocked: no reviewed, independent TOML/JSON or real log-file train-and-validation families here; Rust code is not provided by Rust Book exposition; OTel survey found no `.log` path. Even reviewed prose/config/code bytes would not establish 150M distinct eligible tokens, actual mixture, source-family isolation or 200 independent test cases. New versioned corpus declarations, exact allowlist/denials, content and near-duplicate audit, tokenizer measurements and sealed evaluation are prerequisites; preserve failed v0/v1 gates and artifacts rather than revising them in place.
