## TOKENIZER-1: declared, not fit

The 18 probe IDs and text SHA-256 values had zero overlaps with all 6,058 release documents. These are diagnostics, never fitted input or heldout source facts. [`tokenizer-bakeoff.yaml`](tokenizer-bakeoff.yaml) binds the final release, 16,384/24,576/32,768 candidates, 268,435,456-byte maximum fit sample, validation-only heldout cap 200 per kind, and the same 98%-of-best compression rule. JSON, TOML and real logs lack paired independent source families. C source (51 train documents / 1,994,621 distinct normalized bytes) is explicitly outside the nine bakeoff groups, not invisibly included in fit. Schema-v1's nine-group requirement and its failure record are unchanged.

Distinct retained **train normalized source**: 3,186 content hashes / **19,121,586 UTF-8 bytes**; declared `general_education` domain contributes **0 bytes**, while prose contributes 7,338,227 bytes. A selected tokenizer does not exist: distinct source **token** counts, measured broad-language **token** share, transformed/synthetic train tokens, effective exposures, heldout compression, probe compression, embedding cost and candidate selection are all **unavailable**, not zero. The three-size bakeoff was intentionally **not launched** because the original source-rights surveys explicitly withhold training approval pending full selected-file provenance, fixtures/privacy, obligations and weight/publication review. Freezing a private corpus candidate is not such approval. This supply is only a **PILOT candidate**, not REFERENCE or DATA_RICH; the earlier 150-million-distinct-token proposal remains unproved and the 201,326,592 exposure target is **not** an adopted budget.
