## Independent evaluation and near duplicates

The untouched v0 cards have 96 distinct source passage parents on each source card (fewer unique upstream files) and 100 world IDs on each scenario card. Their v1 scenario generators cycle only 5–9 causal states and include a seed-derived `fixture_id`: extra seed numbers do not produce 200 independent worlds. A new card freezer is deliberately fail-closed on repeated causal worlds, source-file parents across splits and fewer than 200 independent cases; its trial on the old release rejected cross-split repeated world facts, and no v1 cards or receipt were frozen. A new reviewed scenario protocol needs at least 200 genuinely different causal world states **per relevant card**, independently assigned test-only source-file parents and separate template families; adding prompt variants does not meet the gate.
