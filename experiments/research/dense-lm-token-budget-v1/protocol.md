# dense-lm-token-budget-v1: frozen protocol

## Question and controlled comparison

Does extending the promoted dense-lm-v1 learning reference beyond 4,096 updates / 4,194,304 supervised targets reduce held-out loss and change fixed-panel generation? Start with seed 42; only replicate seeds 17 and 73 under the gates below. Source: [dense-lm-v1 acceptance](../../../artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_v1.json), original immutable 4,096-step full-state generations and [lifecycle inputs](../../../configs/dense_lm_v1_lifecycle.json). This is a **state-preserving** continuation of the same AdamW optimizer, FP32 ROCm model and data, not a fresh 16M cosine run. Warmup/cosine ends at step 4,096; all additional updates use floor LR 3e-5. The sole intervention is the number of training targets at that floor. The 4,096-step coordinate uses the promoted observation and checkpoint, never a fresh training run.

Frozen configs: [42](../../../configs/dense_lm_token_budget_v1_seed42.yaml), [17](../../../configs/dense_lm_token_budget_v1_seed17.yaml), [73](../../../configs/dense_lm_token_budget_v1_seed73.yaml). Each parent is the like-seeded dense-lm-v1 terminal generation `step_00004096_gen_000007`; seed 42's checkpoint digest is `b6e77e30cc78731a74dd713471106c4b10afca2901da501b95d30e45c1b1b524`. Run IDs and mutable work belong under `sparselab-work/experiments/dense-lm-token-budget-v1/`, never the source run store. Require source/run/artifact identity verification; audit and explicitly allow source/driver drift only if training/evaluator scientific settings, backend, tokenizer and prepared arrays remain fixed. An incompatible input blocks execution, not grounds for rebuilding the dataset or changing precision.

Milestones are exactly 8,192 updates / 8,388,608 targets and 16,384 / 16,777,216 targets. Stop seed 42 at 8,192 and exercise its verified immutable generation; resume to 16,384 with the **same** 16,384-step config and exercise again. 17/73 are extended only if the preregistered gate triggers. Their 8M endpoints are always exercised if replicated; continue them to 16M if a 16M effect occurred. Training array has 4,402,585 packed tokens: extension repeats the same prepared training data across epochs; record epoch/cursor and distinguish additional target exposures from new documents. Do not alter the validation array or six-case 32-token greedy prompt panel.

## Predeclared independent measurements and decisions

- Report each held-out loss at 4,096, 8,192 and 16,384 and the periodic validation trajectory (same 64 batches / 65,408 held-out targets), with loss gain per added million supervised targets. A **material loss gain** is at least 0.05 nats/target below the seed's promoted 4,096 endpoint at either milestone, **or** at least 0.05 additional decrease from 8,192 to 16,384. Report raw values even if neither threshold is met. Loss is not a proxy for text quality.
- **Observable flattening**, not convergence, requires all three: a positive 4,096→8,192 loss decrease; an 8,192→16,384 decrease smaller than one-quarter of that first decrease despite twice the added tokens; less than 0.01 reduction from the last periodic evaluation at/before step 16,000 to the 16,384 endpoint. Report all deltas even when criterion fails. No claim about broader model capacity follows.
- Independently classify **each** of the six frozen greedy outputs for nonempty/mechanical validity and unchanged/improved/worse text versus the promoted same-seed output. Quote explicit contradictions for Mia/Leo/boat/pond, blue kite/green meadow, and rain/wet path. Compare each case's repeated-token, bigram and trigram excess and distinct-token ratio. Preserve regressions alongside corrections; do not pool into a quality score. The odd OOD case is descriptive only. The built-in `promotion_eligible` degeneration gate establishes only nonempty outputs and is not this study's gate.
- **Replication trigger:** seed 42 has a material loss gain at either milestone, **or** a clear correction of an existing contradiction in at least one of the three continuity cases, **or** at least five fewer repeated-token excess on at least two in-domain cases. Any trigger at 8M or 16M calls for seed 17/73 8M measurements; a 16M effect also calls for their 16M measurements. If none occurs, stop after seed 42; no promotion or additional budget levels. OOD chat cards do not trigger replication.
- Independently report command elapsed wall, cumulative optimizer update time and throughput, validation/checkpoint/exercise times, peak allocated/reserved VRAM, overflow/retry and unavailable measurements. Historical seed-42 reference: 584.232-second training-command wall and 332.123-second optimizer total at 4,096 updates; predict approximately 332 optimizer seconds per extra 4.19M targets under unchanged throughput, plus evaluations, checkpoints and process starts. Compare measured and forecast intervals without conflating wall and update time.

Before any new training freeze hashes of this protocol, three configs, fixed panel, parent manifests and original source revision in [preregistration](preregistration.md). Publish observations and interpretations separately in `results.md` and [acceptance JSON](../../../artifacts/acceptance/dense_lm_token_budget_v1.json). Keep failed, negative, censored or interrupted evidence and do not revise this protocol after viewing outcomes.
