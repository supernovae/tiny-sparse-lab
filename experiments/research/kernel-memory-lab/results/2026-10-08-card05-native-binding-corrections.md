- **Native execution:** `sparselab attempt init/status/run` exposes the existing persistent v2 ledger. `attempt run` binds one phase to a direct native train receipt or one explicitly selected Campaign run, panel or nongenerative evaluation stage. Campaign `apply --only-stage` stops after that stage and rejects a contracted stage without the matching reserved ledger phase; contracted runs require an owned local runtime profile, refusing worker bindings whose descendants the local monitor cannot own. Before spawn, the adapter checks the selected Campaign action, pinned scientific lock and contract, config or panel maxima, and the common-root binding. Direct training pins the exact config bytes. After success it cold-verifies native terminal checkpoint/progress counters (subtracting an authenticated resume parent), panel attempt token IDs, or Campaign output receipts; only these verified values can populate actual ledger counters. A failed or unverifiable phase retains its full reservation. Native train suppresses its otherwise implicit post-train diagnostic generations during a ledger-bound train phase, because that phase reserves zero generation calls.
