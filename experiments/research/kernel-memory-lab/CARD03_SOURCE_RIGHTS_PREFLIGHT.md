The inference that a **private review sample** is feasible follows from these source terms and the absence of a source-level prohibition in the inspected material; it is not a legal clearance of every row. The source declarations conservatively set `training_eligibility` and `redistribution_mode` to `review_required`; both release views are unselected. The acquisition must preserve excluded and unavailable rows, verify both upstream LFS SHA-256 claims against local bytes, and stop on any metadata/identity, checksum, rights, transport, decompression, retained-output, disk or deadline cap. Source-card reads for this preflight precede the native acquisition attempt; its budget starts at `corpus budget-init` and accounts for all native metadata/redirect/error and shard response bodies from that point.

The accepted [pilot limits](CARD03_PILOT_PROPOSAL.md) are **1,323,415,169 charged source-body bytes**, **1,048,576 metadata-body bytes**, **1,324,463,745 combined response-body bytes**, **2,700 seconds**, **5 GiB additional disk** and **1,000 inodes**, with one interruption-only retry. The native declaration adds a conservative **2 GiB expanded-stream cap per shard** and retains at most **32 MiB/32 rows per shard** from at most **4,096 scanned rows**. Expanded-stream bytes are not retained disk bytes. No model target tokens or optimizer updates are allocated. The 64 MiB S1 pilot is superseded and cannot be run.

PagerDuty remains outside this acquisition. Its pinned 36 Markdown files still need file-level rights/exclusion review and a transfer bound before a domain-source attempt. This two-shard pilot cannot itself satisfy Card 03's full source, split, main-tokenizer or 200/400-item evaluation gate.
