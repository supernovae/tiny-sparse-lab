## Language mixture and exposure identity

Propose a base-calibration target of **65% general prose, 25% explanatory prose and 10% incident-response prose by actual training target tokens**. The first two strata may use different rights-cleared Common Pile components; the last uses admitted PagerDuty documents. Code is optional and has zero planned share. This is a target for a later approved token budget, not a measured corpus or an automatic property of `requested_mixture`.

After source admission, freeze source family IDs and a tokenizer identity. Form complete-document train records, then sort within each stratum by SHA-256 of `kml-card03-v1 | stratum | document_id`. A later approved materialization operation must interleave these records against the three target-token quotas while recording every selected document, its order, encoded target count, repeat count, source and shortfall. No silent replacement from another stratum; no validation/test record can fill a train quota. Record requested shares, realized shares, total encoded targets, usable blocks, objective-masked targets and exposure passes separately. A tokenizer or source change creates a new mixture identity. The current Corpus Forge `requested_mixture` is reporting metadata, not an enacted sampler; see the conditional code gap in `TODO.md`.

## Split, deduplication and leakage rules

Assign **source families before chunking or question writing**. A family binds all revisions, mirrors, paraphrases and derived chunks of the same book, document series, repository topic cluster or synthetic scenario. Choose train, validation and final-test family inventories from immutable source metadata with a fixed `kml-card03-v1` split seed, stratified by source/stratum; target 80/10/10 by eligible family count, then freeze actual family lists and denominators. If a small source cannot supply independent families for each needed stratum, mark that stratum unavailable rather than moving a viewed outcome. Hold out entire incident-response document families and entities for the evidence suite. For synthetic cases, split by template family, composition pattern, scenario/world and entity set before generating paraphrases, translations or counterfactual siblings.

Normalize Unicode to NFC and whitespace only for screening; retain original bytes and their SHA-256. Remove exact cross-split duplicates by content digest. Use native `corpus near-duplicates` at its declared `min_jaccard=0.65` screening threshold on a frozen release to flag cross-split three-word-shingle candidates, then review flagged pairs and source siblings before accepting a split; its candidate search is not a complete leakage proof. Reject or quarantine unresolved near duplicates, answer-bearing passage overlap or family overlap. Do not repair a frozen release in place: revise the split declaration and make a new release. Run native `memorization analyze` as a bounded descriptive overlap check, never as leakage clearance or a quality score. Compare evaluation prompts, question-answer pairs, hidden rationales and distinctive quoted passages against training and tokenizer-fit inventories; none may be copied into them. Ordinary answer words may occur in training prose, so lexical overlap alone is not a failure. In the designated **open-book** condition, held-out reference documents may be present as evidence; question-answer pairs and hidden answers must not enter model training, tokenizer fitting, retrieval keys or query construction.

Use stable raw document and chunk IDs bound to source revision, section/span, chunking version and raw-text digest. Retain line/byte offsets, license evidence and source-version precedence. Gold chunk IDs must resolve to source text; array positions are not identities. Store future insertion/correction records outside train and initial retrieval stores until their declared test.

## Main tokenizer contract

Propose a new **32,768-entry byte-level BPE** tokenizer, never the Card 02 fixture tokenizer. The native trainer has fixed `<pad>`, `<unk>`, `<bos>`, `<eos>` IDs 0–3, ByteLevel pre-tokenization and decoding, and rejects an actual vocabulary size different from the request. Fit only on admitted **train-family** text after splitting and deduplication; validation, test and evidence answers are excluded. Pin `schema_version: 1`, `vocab_size: 32768`, `min_frequency: 2`, train document/UTF-8-byte ceilings, exact source manifest, output path, source revision and selected document digest in the later declaration. The native `train_max_tokens` field is used as a tokenizer **input-byte** bound here; do not describe it as an observed model target-token count.

Before selecting the main tokenizer, verify the artifact manifest and exact special IDs; require exact encode/decode round trips for ordinary ASCII, punctuation, newlines, tabs, composed/decomposed Unicode, non-Latin scripts and emoji; separately specify handling of literal special-token strings. Require zero `<unk>` for valid held-out UTF-8 text and report bytes/token, tokens/character, length tails and sequence-overflow rate by held-out source family and by the language/evidence suites. Compare train and held-out distributions without fitting or tuning on held-out items. Any round-trip or special-ID failure blocks selection; a tokenizer that underfills 32,768 entries fails rather than silently shrinking the model vocabulary. Record coverage gaps and an explicit decision before using a tokenizer with poor held-out efficiency. The shipped `corpus tokenizer-bakeoff` can provide train/held-out family receipts after a separately approved 256 MiB fit; that fixed bakeoff budget is **not** authorized by this specification or the small preparation pilot below.

## Frozen evaluation protocol, before any capability run

Version the **language diagnostic** as 200 closed-book items: 20 each for paraphrase, reference/coreference, negation, quantifier/scope, conditional reasoning, temporal ordering, compositional inference, answer constraints, ambiguity/needed clarification, and contradiction or insufficient information. It tests broad reading behavior without a domain evidence store. Version the **evidence suite** as 400 open-book items: 40 each for direct supported extraction, paraphrased support, reference resolution, negation/scope, conditions/exceptions, temporal or version precedence, two-source inference, contradiction, multi-part constraints, and missing/ambiguous evidence. Within the last group, predeclare separate missing and clarification denominators. These counts are design targets, **not materialized items or statistical-power claims**.

Every item needs stable ID, suite/protocol version, parent family, split, source and chunk versions, question text, allowed answer length, answerability, required and prohibited atomic claims, acceptable paraphrases or numeric tolerances, support chunk IDs, precedence rule, clarification or abstention target, rubric category, reviewer and content hash. Freeze the item manifest and denominator before inspecting model outputs. For each answerable evidence item, compare the same query, evidence-token budget and decoder in no-evidence, gold, plausible-wrong and shuffled/absent conditions; mark inapplicable conditions explicitly for unanswerable items. Score supported correctness, unsupported claims, citation/source support and appropriate abstention/clarification separately. Accept multiple semantically correct answers; do not use string exact match as the sole criterion. Require blind independent adjudication for ambiguous cases and preserve disagreements. Report per-category numerators/denominators and missing/unavailable results; no aggregate score hides a failed category.

Evaluation questions, hidden answers and rubrics are authored only from held-out families or original fictional cases. A retrieval query may use visible question text and permitted context, never a gold answer or future observation. Keep the future raw-text oracle, lexical retrieval and integrated reader conditions bound to identical source versions and chunks. A passing tiny fixture or tokenizer coverage check is not a comprehension result.
