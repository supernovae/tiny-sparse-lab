The panel uses the exact `Question:` text in the checked-in prompts, no evidence chunks or few-shot exemplars, greedy decoding (`temperature=0`, `top_k=0`, seed 17), native KV cache, and at most 128 new tokens. Offline tokenizer verification found the longest exact prompt plus its reserved completion is **213/1,024 tokens**. The native panel has no stop sequences; generated role delimiters or extra turns are retained as observed formatting failures.

For each exact frozen item, two reviewers independently judge the raw completion against its `answerability`, `required_claims`, `prohibited_claims`, `acceptable_paraphrases`, `numeric_tolerance`, `clarification_target`, item-specific answer-length limit and explicit question constraints. Unicode NFC/casefold/trimmed whitespace normalizes surface variation; acceptable paraphrases are examples, not an exclusive string list. Score **1** only if an answerable response entails every required claim, affirms no prohibited or unsupported material claim and obeys constraints; a `clarify` response asks for the declared missing detail without inventing an answer; an `unanswerable` response explicitly abstains without fabricating a fact. Otherwise score **0**. Empty completions score 0. Resolve reviewer disagreements with a recorded third adjudication against the frozen rubric; never edit item golds or prompts after seeing outputs. If a row lacks a durable completion or its review remains unresolved at the cap, the 200-item eligibility score is **unestablished**, not extrapolated.

Operationally, **stable instruction formatting** means at least **190/200** responses and at least **18/20 on each axis** are nonempty, contain one answer or one clarifying question only, do not echo the prompt, introduce role delimiters/another turn, or exceed the item's answer-length and 128-token caps. Record formatting separately from semantic correctness; a format failure also scores 0 on that item. Proposed reader eligibility requires **all 200 items scored**, at least **120/200 correct overall**, at least **10/20 correct on each of the ten language axes**, finite selected-checkpoint held-out loss, stable formatting by the operational rule above, and verified input/checkpoint/resource receipts. Failure is a negative result; incomplete evaluation or budget exhaustion leaves eligibility unestablished.
