# TinyStories dense 30M data-rich v1: bounded finding

**Finding (learning; not promoted).** One seed-42, 29,893,120-parameter dense run reached its frozen 24,576-update / 100,663,296-supervised-target budget with a verified full-state endpoint. On the complete final 8,000-story held-out split, native-tokenizer teacher-forced loss was **1.610636 nats/target**, perplexity **5.005992**, across **1,599,632 targets** (including one EOS per story). On the same preregistered 256 raw stories, bits per UTF-8 byte were **0.668729 new**, **0.975226 old 30M**, **0.955759 old 50M**. This is an observed same-text likelihood difference, not a controlled improvement attributable to any one training factor or a verdict on story quality.

All likelihood panels had **zero failed stories**, score every encoded raw-text target exactly once, include an EOS start context and EOS end target, and use model-native tokenizers. Different tokens/target denominators and training protocols prohibit cross-tokenizer NLL/target or perplexity as an apples-to-apples baseline comparison. Same-text bits/byte is comparable as measured text likelihood, not a tokenizer ablation; the held-out 512/256 and common-control 128/64 numbers also have different context and population.

All **6 original greedy regression prompts × 3 models**, **22 decoder-development prompts × 3 models × 3 policies/seeds**, and **55 opened decoder-test prompts × 3 models × 3 policies/seeds** completed with raw text, IDs, checkpoint identity, timings and mechanical diagnostics. All 55 test prompts fell in each model's native <=256-token subset, so that subset is the same cells, not another independent trial. On the opened test panel, greedy repeated-trigram excess summed to **233 old 30M / 200 old 50M / 160 new** over 55 prompts; temperature 0.8 (two fixed seeds per prompt) yielded **52 / 73 / 70** over 110 cells. Those unequal cell counts and repetitions are diagnostics, not a quality ranking. The new model produced **2 greedy and 2 sampled cells** flagged with explicit color-attribute contradictions; for example, the expected green ribbon became yellow in `test-color-attribute-continuity-01` greedy. Negative cells remain in the raw record.

The new-only long-context panel (four development and four test prompts, 257–512 native prompt tokens; 3 fixed cells each) completed without generation errors. Every long-test cell stopped before 48 new tokens, including one two-token sampled completion; this is descriptive, not evidence of reliable long-range reference tracking. No old-model matched long-context comparison exists. Hand-authored test prompts are not certified train-disjoint, and neither mechanical anchors nor n-grams replace blinded human review.

The automatically published post-train triage (`sparselab-work/experiments/tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1/runs/tinystories-dense-30m-data-rich-v1-seed42/post-train-triage/1670540c7fb28ac02c88f569506cd2e6e8ff1532bded3a4b02488ebb1f08e6f2.json`) reports integrity **PASS**, a recorded sampled-loss trend **still improving**, and `DECODER_SENSITIVITY_DETECTED`; endpoint-learning and plateau triggers remain **UNKNOWN** because four comparable weight-verified loss points are unavailable. It recommends a **new preregistered independent behavior/decoder study before further training**, not automatic selection or promotion. The pinned upstream `Evaluation prompts.yaml` was downloaded and its first-ten prefix hash recorded in ignored `evaluation/upstream-reference-receipt.json`, but this optional, not-train-disjoint panel was **not generated**; no external public-model comparator was run. Neither can silently enlarge the sealed decision population.

**Decision:** retain this single-seed result as a bounded learning finding and unpromoted lifecycle entry. Source diversity, BPE, context, schedule, and BF16 precision changed together against old references; do not infer which caused likelihood differences, model superiority in prose, convergence, seed reliability, or cross-device reproducibility. Reopen only under a new preregistered, independently reviewed behavior protocol; preserve this opened test set and every negative output.
