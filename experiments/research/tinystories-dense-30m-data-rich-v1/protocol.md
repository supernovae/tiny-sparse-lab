Status: preregistered, not a baseline promotion. Parent: dense-lm-v1. ## Source and selection

Snapshot exact source ordinals, content/file SHA-256, bytes, counts and duplicates; exclusive, immutable and offline-replayable. Train gate >=750,000 fully retained distinct stories AND >=100,000,000 packed training IDs; goal >=120,000,000 IDs. If <120M IDs, only a *new versioned snapshot* extending original train to at most 1,500,000 unique stories by 100,000 increments is allowed, rebinding every dependent hash. No lowering the gates or substituting a backend. First 2,000 validation documents are tokenizer-development-only; final 8,000 never select tokenizer.

Fit ByteLevel BPE on identical first 250,000 snapshot train stories with a whole-document 256 MiB UTF-8 ceiling, min_frequency=2, no prefix space, special IDs pad/unk/bos/eos=0/1/2/3. Candidate requested vocabulary sizes: 8,192, 12,000, 16,384. Report held-out development untruncated token counts (excluding EOS), tokens/UTF-8 byte, median/p90/p99 per story, fraction >512, UTF-8 roundtrip, manifest verification, artifact bytes and fixed-device encoding throughput. Exclude any invalid special-ID/roundtrip/manifest candidate; choose smallest vocabulary with development token/byte <=1.02 times the valid minimum; exact ties smallest. Freeze receipt before model losses or generations. Unigram not admitted.

## Training

Tied dense decoder hidden=512, layers=8, heads=8, FFN determined by selected vocabulary: 8192→1408; 12000→1248; 16384→1072. Verify canonical ~30M *stored* parameters via inspect. Seed 42; model and training context 512. Effective batch 8; 24,576 optimizer steps and 100,663,296 supervised exposures (4,096 per step), max_tokens=100663296. Deterministic AdamW peak=0.0003, floor=0.00003, betas=(0.9,0.95), decay=0.1, clipping=1.0, warmup 256, decay horizon 24576. Evaluate every 2048 steps and endpoint, max 128 fixed validation batches. Full-state scheduled checkpoint steps 0/1024/2048/4096/8192/12288/16384/20480/24576 plus every new best and latest/previous verified. Count supervised exposures separately from distinct packed IDs, blocks and epochs.

On the provisioned ROCm RX 7900 XTX, fixed short FP32/BF16 pilots (if BF16 supported) at 8×1, 4×2, 2×4, 1×8. Same context/effective batch; post-initialization throughput distribution, finiteness, peak allocation, reload, headroom. BF16 requires >=10% speed improvement and >=20% measured VRAM headroom; otherwise FP32. Choose fastest stable pair; ties 8×1. Freeze pilot receipt/config before full run. Inspect→validate→disposable smoke→warmup, fit/resource forecast including all scheduled and new-best generations, inflight write, cache, and >=25 GiB/20% untouched storage. Keep distinct prep, optimizer, validation, checkpoint, generation, reporting durations. No concurrent GPU tasks.
